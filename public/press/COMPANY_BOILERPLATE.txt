ABOUT SENTINEL

Sentinel is a vine-level vineyard management platform built on RTK GPS technology. Operating like digital flagging tape, Sentinel creates a permanent, sub-centimeter-accurate record for every vine on the property. Vineyard crews carry a handheld GPS unit and tag each vine with health status, disease flags, replant decisions, and custom attributes as they walk the rows.

The result is a living digital map of the vineyard where every plant has its own history. Managers can query by any attribute -- block, age cohort, production status, disease flag -- and generate work orders, compliance reports, or data exports directly from the results. The system replaces clipboard-based scouting with GPS-verified records that persist across seasons, crew changes, and ownership transitions.

Founded by vineyard technology practitioners, Sentinel serves premium estate vineyards and vineyard management companies in California, Oregon, and internationally.

PRODUCT OVERVIEW

- Vine-by-vine GPS mapping at sub-centimeter accuracy (RTK)
- Mobile field app for iOS with offline-first architecture
- Disease tracking, replant planning, and work order generation
- Data export to Excel with full query filtering
- Multi-property, multi-user access with role-based permissions
